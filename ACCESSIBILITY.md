# PayScope Accessibility Statement

PayScope aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA across its public website and signed-in workspace. This is the standard we use for improvements. Full conformance across all pages and assistive technologies is still being assessed. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)

## Using PayScope

- Use **Skip to content** to jump past navigation. Links and buttons show a focus indicator during keyboard use.
- PayScope respects reduced-motion preferences in its landing page and workspace transitions.
- Charts and relationship graphs may be harder to use with assistive technology or at high zoom. The **Payments** table lists dates, descriptions and amounts, and **Subscriptions** lists the recurring payments behind your estimates.

## Report an accessibility barrier

Share the page, the task you were trying to finish, and what got in your way. Your browser and assistive technology, if applicable, can help us investigate. Use sample data or redact names, account references and balances from anything you share. [Report an accessibility issue on GitHub](https://github.com/vinaybhadane/PayScope/issues/new?title=Accessibility%3A%20)

## Verify changes

Check the keyboard tab order, visible focus, skip link, headings, and reflow at narrow widths. Use a screen reader for manual testing and automated checks to catch common regressions. See the website statement for users and the repository’s previous remediation findings.
